## The Need

Machine learning has shown great potential to revolutionize everything from how farmers increase crop yields, to how governments communicate during natural disasters, to how healthcare providers respond to global pandemics. But in low- and middle-income contexts globally, a lack of unbiased training and evaluation data puts those benefits out of reach.

In many cases, data required to build AI applications for real-world problems does not exist. Where it does, it is often outdated, missing key information, or not representative of underserved populations—leading to bias and decreased accuracy. Machine learning tools then learn these biases, which can lead to harmful outcomes for people of color, women, and other marginalized populations.

## The world's first collaborative effort

Lacuna Fund provides resources to produce new datasets for underserved populations or problems, augment datasets to be more representative, or update old datasets to be more sustainable. Guided by machine learning professionals worldwide, the Fund is designed for and by the communities it will serve.

All datasets produced are locally developed and owned, openly accessible to the international community, and adhere to best practices in ethics and privacy.

[Read the launch press release](https://www.dsfsi.co.za/lacunafund-mirror/wp-content/uploads/sites/11/2020/07/Lacuna-Fund_-Launch-Press-Release-Web.pdf).

## Our Grantmaking

The Steering Committee focuses Lacuna Fund grantmaking on the creation, expansion, and maintenance of training and evaluation data in four areas: agriculture, health, language, and climate. We accept applications from non-profit entities, research institutions, for-profit social enterprises, and teams of such organizations through requests for proposals.

## Lacuna Fund aims to

- Disburse funds to create, expand, and maintain datasets that fill gaps and reduce bias.
- Enable underserved populations to benefit from advances in AI.
- Deepen understanding of how to fund equitably developed training and evaluation data effectively.
