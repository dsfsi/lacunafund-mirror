## Training and scaling ML models for the Global South

Lacuna Fund and the African Centre for Technology Studies (ACTS), with support from Wellcome Trust, invite proposals that turn existing Lacuna Fund datasets into equitable, high-value machine learning models, applications, and tools.

<div class="cards"><div class="card"><h3>Funding</h3><p>Up to USD 30,000 — three awards of up to USD 10,000 each.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Eligible applicants</h3><p>Researchers and research teams in Global South LMICs.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Implementation</h3><p>December 2026 – May 2027.</p></div></div>

## Who can apply

- Non-profit entities, research institutions, for-profit social enterprises, or teams of these organisations. Individuals apply through an institutional sponsor.
- Lead applicants must be headquartered in a low- or middle-income country in the Global South; institutions elsewhere may participate as partners.
- Applicants must demonstrate competency in machine learning and their proposed SDG domain.
- Applicants must have or be able to obtain national and other required approvals.

## What we will support

Proposals may be submitted in English, Spanish, French, or Portuguese. Projects must use Lacuna Fund datasets, deliver at least a pilot-stage minimum viable product, and provide openly licensed outputs with clear documentation.

- Train and deploy a new ML model using existing Lacuna Fund datasets.
- Scale or fine-tune an existing model for a local or regional context.
- Build decision-support tools, predictive applications, APIs, dashboards, or visualisations.
- Develop baseline models or train future data-science professionals.
