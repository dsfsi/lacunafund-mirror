<div class="callout"><h3>Advancing SDG outcomes through open datasets</h3><p>Funding is available for Global South teams to train, scale, and apply ML models using Lacuna Fund datasets. Full proposals are due 13 November 2026.</p><a class="button" href="{{ '/rfp/' | relative_url }}">View the RFP</a></div>

## Who can apply

- Non-profit entities
- Research institutions
- For-profit social enterprises
- Teams of such organizations

## Requirements

- Apply through a Lacuna Fund request for proposals.
- Have the technical capacity for dataset labelling, creation, expansion, or maintenance.
- Ensure datasets are locally developed and owned.
- Follow ethical labour standards and protect privacy and prevent harm.
- Use appropriate open-data licensing for datasets and related intellectual property.

## How the RFP process works

1. An independent Technical Advisory Panel identifies data gaps and refines the RFP focus.
2. Lacuna Fund releases the request for proposals.
3. Panel members review and select proposals.
4. ACTS disburses grants as Secretariat.
5. Locally owned, openly accessible datasets are published and learnings shared.
