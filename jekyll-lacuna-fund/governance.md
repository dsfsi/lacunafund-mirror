## Guiding Principles

Lacuna Fund is designed for and by the communities it will serve. Its governance is rooted in accessibility, equity, ethics, participatory approach, quality, and transformational impact.

<div class="cards"><div class="card"><h3>Accessibility</h3><p>Data created through funding are accessible to and benefit underserved communities, using appropriate open licensing.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Equity</h3><p>Datasets should represent the Global South and people of color globally without creating or reinforcing bias.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Ethics</h3><p>Funded collection follows ethical labour standards and protects privacy and prevents harm.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Participatory approach</h3><p>Local beneficiaries and end users participate in governance and data creation.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Quality</h3><p>Data should enable beneficial applications in research, communities, and industry.</p></div><div class="card"><h3>Transformational impact</h3><p>The Fund addresses fundamental data gaps to unlock AI advances for underserved communities.</p></div></div>
